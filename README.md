# Escaping Feather
*A simple and fun vertical launch tool for players – perfect for small servers!*

Created by **NoNameDude aka xXNicoXx**

---

## 📦 Description

The **Escaping Feather Mod** adds a special tool that lets players **leap into the air** with a powerful vertical boost and cool particle effects! Great for quick escapes, fun movement, or adding flair to your server's lobby or minigames.

Perfectly lightweight and designed for **small Minetest servers** or personal worlds. No dependencies, no complexity – just plug and play.
